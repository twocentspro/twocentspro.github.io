Seeking wisdom - Fear of the Lord

The Bible is a rich source of wisdom, offering guidance and insights for living a meaningful and righteous life. Here are some examples of wisdom found in the Bible:


1. Fear of the Lord: Proverbs 9:10 states, "The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom, and the knowledge of the Holy One is insight." Recognizing God's authority and seeking to live in accordance with His teachings lays the foundation for acquiring true wisdom.

2. Seeking God's guidance: Proverbs 3:5-6 advises, "Trust in the LORD with all your heart, and do not lean on your own understanding. In all your ways acknowledge him, and he will make straight your paths." Seeking God's wisdom through prayer, studying His Word, and relying on His guidance leads to wise decision-making.

3. Humility: Proverbs 11:2 states, "When pride comes, then comes disgrace, but with the humble is wisdom." Recognizing our limitations, acknowledging our need for God's wisdom, and treating others with humility allows us to gain true wisdom.

4. Pursuit of knowledge: Proverbs 18:15 encourages us, "An intelligent heart acquires knowledge, and the ear of the wise seeks knowledge." The Bible emphasizes the importance of seeking knowledge, understanding, and wisdom through continual learning, reflection, and study of God's Word.


5. Discernment: The book of Proverbs is filled with teachings on discernment, emphasizing the ability to distinguish between right and wrong, to make wise choices, and to avoid the pitfalls of foolishness. Proverbs 14:15 states, "The simple believes everything, but the prudent gives thought to his steps."

6. Wise counsel: Proverbs 15:22 advises, "Without counsel plans fail, but with many advisers they succeed." Seeking wise and godly counsel from trusted individuals who have walked the path of faith can provide valuable insights and guidance.

7. Integrity and righteousness: The pursuit of integrity and righteousness is a theme woven throughout the Bible. Proverbs 11:3 declares, "The integrity of the upright guides them, but the crookedness of the treacherous destroys them." Living a life of integrity and righteousness aligns with God's wisdom and leads to blessings.

8. Love and compassion: The wisdom literature of the Bible emphasizes the importance of love and compassion in relationships and society. Proverbs 19:22 states, "What is desired in a man is steadfast love, and a poor man is better than a liar." Love and compassion are foundational virtues that guide our interactions with others and reflect God's wisdom.

These are just a few examples of the wisdom found throughout the Bible. The Scriptures provide guidance, principles, and insights that, when applied to our lives, lead to a path of wisdom, righteousness, and a deepening relationship with God.
