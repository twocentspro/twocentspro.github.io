THREE RULES OF CC INVESTING.

Target annual rate of return: 15 to 20+ %. 

These rules are designed to minimise risk and maximise gains. These rules are easy to understand and not hard to implement.

  1. Don't lose money
    • Doing nothing means losing money as inflation is unavoidable
    • Avoid unnecessary financial transaction related fees (save up to 3% of fees)
    • Avoid get-rich-quick schemes
      • Good planning and hard work lead to prosperity, but hasty shortcuts lead to poverty. – Proverbs 
      • Keep emotion out of investing. Skills, discipline and patience are the keys to investing.
  2. Buy good companies with a proven moat / value model
    • Do you understand the product or service offered by the company?
    • Will people still be using this product or service in 5 years?
    • Key to Growth: Invest in companies focused on innovation which can make the world a better place.
  3. Hold if there is a proven time value model
    • Has the company had consistent earnings growth? Generally the EPS growth must be at least 10%?
    • We will guide you on how to play with house money (not gamble). You will learn:
      • The Consistent Certainty approach to pick quality stocks to bring you wealth
      • Moderate dividend based blue clips with growth potential.
      • A proactive approach: sell covered call options to generate additional income / cash flow.
