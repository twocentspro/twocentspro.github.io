Why Tesla as of 2021 Q2

FSD · TESLA

As of the second quarter of 2021, the bull case for Tesla came down to a handful of straightforward observations:

  • Unit and revenue growth above 30%.
  • Accelerated growth from entering new markets.
  • Best-in-class products.
  • Short-term tailwinds still in motion.
  • Long-term megatrends starting to bear fruit.

And yet the company remained deeply misunderstood — and was still viewed as expensive as of the beginning of April, 2021. Whether that price was justified depended entirely on which of those two views you believed.
